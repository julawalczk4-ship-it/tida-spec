# Vision

## Why Tida exists

I was a competitive kayaker for over ten years, training in a group with men. Women were often treated like weaker boys — not like professionals with a different instruction manual.

While writing my master's thesis on gender equality in sport, I found research mapping hormones and training potential to each phase of the cycle. It showed that women can train smarter — not less — when training follows the cycle.

Tida turns that into practical, daily guidance.

## The problem

Most training and nutrition plans assume a woman's body is the same every day of the month. It isn't. Energy, strength and recovery shift across the cycle.

The result for active women:
- pushing hard on low-energy days → frustration, sense of "failing" the plan
- risk of overtraining
- no guidance on *when* to go hard and *when* to recover

## Who Tida is for

**Primary user:** an active woman who trains seriously — from gym and running to outdoor and competitive sport — and wants to train smarter, in tune with her body. Including women who can't afford to "just rest" for a few days every month: athletes and women with demanding jobs.

She:
- is aware and curious about her body, not a beginner to exercise
- is frustrated by plans that ignore her natural energy dips
- wants practical, daily guidance — not theory

**Not for:** people looking for medical diagnosis, treatment, or fertility/conception tracking.

## What Tida does

One promise: **"Here's what makes sense for your body today."**

Every day the user gets guidance in three areas, based on her current cycle phase:
1. **Training** — e.g. strong cardio / strength vs. yoga / mobility
2. **Nutrition** — what supports her body in this phase
3. **Recovery** — e.g. sauna, rest, sleep focus

Plus she can track **progress in the context of how she feels**, so she sees patterns over time.

## Positioning

**Sport · fitness · lifestyle · wellbeing — not a health or medical app.**

This is a deliberate choice (see `decisions.md` → D2). It shapes copy, features and what we never claim.

## What Tida is not

- ❌ Not medical advice or diagnosis
- ❌ Not a contraception or fertility tool
- ❌ Not a generic workout app with a calendar bolted on

**Health, nutrition and contraception settings:** the user can tell Tida about health conditions, diet and contraception. Tida uses this only as context for its recommendations — it never diagnoses, treats, or works as a contraception or fertility tool. How each setting changes the recommendations: see `open-questions.md` → Q6.

## How we'll know it works

> ⚠️ Draft metrics — to validate after launch

- User opens the daily recommendation on most days of the week
- User reports better match between plan and energy (in-app check-in)
- Retention across at least 2–3 full cycles (one cycle is too short to see value)
